Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

The short version: DD & Bri is a show for young children, and we take their privacy seriously. This website has no accounts, no sign-ups, no tracking, no cookies and no ads. We do not collect any personal information from children. If you email us, we use your email only to reply.

Who we are

DD & Bri (ddandbri.com and the DD & Bri YouTube channel, @DDandBriTV) is operated by David M Jacobs ("we", "us"). You can reach us at info@ddandbri.com.

Children's privacy

DD & Bri is made for children ages 2–6. We comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect, use or share personal information from children under 13. This website does not ask children for any information, has no chat or comment features, and has no accounts.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent us personal information (for example, by email), contact us at info@ddandbri.com and we will delete it promptly.

Information we collect on this website

  • None automatically. We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising tags, tracking pixels or third-party fonts. All images and fonts are served from this website.
  • Hosting logs. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure its service. We do not use these logs to identify or track visitors.
  • Emails you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply to you. We do not add you to any mailing list or share your email with anyone.

Watching on YouTube

DD & Bri episodes are published on YouTube, and every video is designated "made for kids". When you or your child watch on YouTube, YouTube's own terms and Google's privacy practices apply, including YouTube's protections for content made for kids. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Links from this website to YouTube open YouTube's site.

Our use of Google and YouTube APIs

We operate a private production tool called Animay that uses the YouTube Data API to publish DD & Bri episodes. With our own authorization, Animay signs in to our own DD & Bri channel only, to upload our videos, set their thumbnails, titles and descriptions, and set their visibility. Every video is reviewed and approved by a person before it is made public.

  • Animay does not access any other person's Google or YouTube account or data, and it is not available to the public.
  • The access tokens Animay receives from Google are stored securely on our own computer, are used only for the purposes above, and are never sold, shared or transferred to anyone.
  • Animay's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's security settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal information with anyone.

Future shop and subscriptions

We plan to offer DD & Bri merchandise and optional paid content in the future. Purchases and subscriptions will be made by parents and guardians, not children. Before any of these features launch, we will update this policy to explain exactly what information is collected (for example, for payment and shipping), how it is used, and who processes it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you (if any) or ask us to delete it by emailing info@ddandbri.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
